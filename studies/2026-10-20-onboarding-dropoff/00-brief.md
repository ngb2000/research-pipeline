# Brief: Why new members abandon mobile account opening

## Objective
A credit union sees 38 percent of people who start opening a checking account in its mobile app stop before funding the account. We want to know where in the flow they stop, why, and what would have kept them going, so the product team can decide what to fix first.

## Audience
Adults who started opening a checking account with the credit union in the last 90 days through the mobile app, the website, or in a branch. We want people who finished and people who abandoned, so the two can be compared.

## Research questions
RQ1. At which step do people abandon account opening, and what happens at that step that makes them stop?
RQ2. How do the reasons for abandoning differ between the app, the website, and the branch?
RQ3. What did people who completed the process do when they hit the same obstacles?
RQ4. What would have brought an abandoner back to finish?

## Constraints
Structured interviews, conducted by phone, 20 to 30 minutes each. Synthetic data for this first run.
